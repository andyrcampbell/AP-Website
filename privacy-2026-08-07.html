1. What information we collect and why

1.1 Enquiry form

When you submit an enquiry through the form on this site, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The nature of your enquiry (edition availability, commission, or other)
  • The title of the work or edition you are interested in (optional)
  • Your message

We use this information solely to respond to your enquiry personally and to conduct any follow-on correspondence about your interest in the work. We do not add you to the collector list or any other register as a result of your submitting an enquiry, without your separate and explicit agreement.

Enquiry submissions are transmitted to us through Formspree, Inc., a form processing service. Formspree receives your name, email address, enquiry details, and message in order to deliver your submission to us. Formspree also processes your IP address as part of its standard service operation. Formspree hosts its infrastructure on Amazon Web Services in the United States. Its privacy practices are described at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

1.2 Collector list registration

If you register on the homepage to receive advance notification of new edition releases, we collect your email address. We use it only to send the notifications described at the point of registration: new edition releases and exhibition appearances. That is the full scope of what this list is for.

Collector list registrations are transmitted to us through Formspree, Inc. Formspree receives your email address and delivers the registration to us. Formspree also processes your IP address as part of its standard service operation. Formspree hosts its infrastructure on Amazon Web Services in the United States. Its privacy practices are described at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

You may withdraw from the collector list at any time. Every notification email includes an unsubscribe link. You may also contact us directly at the address in Section 14, and we will remove your address from the list and confirm that we have done so. We will honour any opt-out request within ten business days.

1.3 Payment transactions via Stripe — forthcoming

The site currently operates on an enquiry basis only. There is no online payment pathway at launch. When an online acquisition pathway is activated, edition acquisitions will be processed through Stripe Payment Links. Clicking an acquisition link will take you to a Stripe-hosted payment page. All payment card processing will be handled by Stripe, Inc. We will not receive, store, or process your card number, bank details, or full billing information at any point. We will receive from Stripe a transaction confirmation containing your name, email address, the edition purchased, and the amount paid — used to fulfil your order and issue your Certificate of Authenticity.

Stripe’s privacy practices are described at stripe.com/privacy. This notice will be updated when the payment pathway goes live.

1.4 Web fonts — Google Fonts

Our site loads display typefaces (Cormorant Garamond and Jost) from Google Fonts, a font delivery service operated by Google LLC. When your browser loads a page on this site, it makes a request to Google’s servers to retrieve these font files. That request discloses your IP address to Google. We do not send any other information about your visit to Google through this mechanism, and we do not receive data about individual visitors from Google’s font service.

Google’s privacy practices are described at policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Edition records on the Hedera network — forthcoming

The studio does not currently operate an on-chain edition record capability. The Hedera minting function is not yet live at launch. When it is introduced, the studio intends, on finalisation of each acquisition, to register a digital edition record on the Hedera distributed ledger and transfer it to the purchaser. That record will carry the title of the work, the edition number, and the photographer’s name.

Two characteristics of this record that prospective collectors should understand before finalising any acquisition at the time this feature is active:

The record will be public. The Hedera network is a public distributed ledger. When an edition record is entered, the record and the wallet addresses associated with it will be visible to anyone who queries the ledger. We will not publish your name or contact details as part of the on-chain record, but if you have associated your wallet address with your identity through any other service or platform, that connection may be traceable by others.

The record will be permanent. Data recorded on a public blockchain is permanent by design. Once an edition record has been written to the Hedera network, it cannot be deleted — whether by us or by anyone else. A deletion request made to us cannot reach data already recorded on the ledger.

If either characteristic is a concern for you, we encourage you to raise it with us before finalising any acquisition at the time this feature is active. The Certificate of Authenticity that accompanies each print serves the same provenance function and is not subject to either constraint. This notice will be updated when the on-chain record capability goes live.

10. Your rights

Whatever your location, you may make any of the following requests to us in writing. We respond within 45 days. If a request requires additional time — for example, because it is complex or we receive multiple requests — we will notify you before the initial 45-day period expires and explain the reason for the extension.

Right to know and access. You may ask us what personal information we hold about you and request a copy.

Right to correct. If information we hold about you is inaccurate, you may ask us to correct it.

Right to delete. You may ask us to delete personal information we hold about you. We will do so unless retention is required to complete or record a confirmed acquisition, to comply with a legal obligation such as tax record retention, or to resolve an active dispute. When the on-chain edition record feature described in Section 4 is introduced, records already written to the Hedera network will also fall outside this commitment — blockchain records are permanent by design and cannot be deleted once written.

Right to opt out of the collector list. You may withdraw at any time by following the unsubscribe link in any email we send, or by writing to us directly.

Right to opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. There is nothing to opt out of on that front. If that changes, we will update this notice and provide an opt-out mechanism before it does.

Non-discrimination. We will not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.

How to submit a request. Write to us at the contact details in Section 14 with the subject line “Privacy Request.” Please describe the right you wish to exercise and provide enough information to allow us to identify your records — for example, the email address you used when registering or submitting an enquiry. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests. You may designate an authorised agent to submit a request on your behalf.
